# wordy privacy policy

*Last updated: 29 September 2026*

wordy is a browser extension that suggests weaker and stronger versions of a word. It
does not collect, store or sell any personal data.

## What wordy reads

When you click the wordy icon, it reads the text you have selected in the current tab so
it can fill in the search box. It only does this when you open it, only on the tab you
opened it on, and it keeps no more than a short word or phrase (three words at most). It
reads nothing else on the page.

## What leaves your device

Most words are looked up in wordy's built-in word list, entirely on your device.

If a word isn't in that list, wordy sends that single word to the free
[Datamuse API](https://www.datamuse.com/api/) (`api.datamuse.com`) to find similar words.
Nothing else is sent: no page address, no page content, no identifiers. Datamuse's own
handling of requests is described on its website.

## What wordy stores

Nothing. wordy has no account, no analytics, no cookies and no storage. When you close the
popup, the word you looked up is gone.
